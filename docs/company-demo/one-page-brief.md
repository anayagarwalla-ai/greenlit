# Greenlit

## Turn your SOW into proof.

Greenlit helps U.S. web agencies move a completed milestone from “the staging link is ready” to a focused client decision and an invoice-ready record.

### The problem

Agency teams lose margin preparing screenshots, translating scope into testable language, chasing a reviewer across email and chat, and separating genuine fixes from new work.

### The workflow

1. Import the relevant SOW section.
2. Confirm measurable criteria tied to exact source language.
3. Verify supported promises against an authorized public staging build.
4. Give one named client reviewer the promise, observed result, evidence, and deadline.
5. Retain the decision and hand the approved milestone into the agency’s billing process.

### Why it is different

Greenlit keeps scope, browser observation, client decision, and the billing handoff connected. It does not treat a polished success message as proof when the underlying request failed.

### Design-partner beta

Greenlit is inviting a small number of U.S. web agencies to test one suitable milestone in a bounded 30-day pilot. The first conversation and demo use synthetic information only.

Best fit: 2–50-person agencies, recurring milestone approvals, a named client reviewer, and public HTTPS staging with same-origin assets.

Not a fit: regulated or confidential data, protected/VPN-only previews, legal signature replacement, compliance certification, or guaranteed payment.
