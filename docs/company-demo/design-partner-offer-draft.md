# Design-partner offer draft

This is a recommended operating offer, not approved commercial or legal language.

## Recommended pilot

- **Audience:** U.S. web agencies with 2–50 people.
- **Term:** 30 days from onboarding.
- **Scope:** one agency team, one client milestone at a time, one named client reviewer.
- **Included workflow:** SOW excerpt import, confirmed criteria, public-HTTPS verification, one active review, approval record, and optional Stripe test-mode setup.
- **Usage guardrail:** up to eight retained verification runs during the pilot, subject to the global daily capacity limit.
- **Support:** one onboarding call, one office-hours session, and business-day best-effort support through the monitored support address.
- **Partner contribution:** one kickoff interview, one midpoint feedback call, one closing interview, and prompt notice of blockers.
- **Data boundary:** authorized U.S. business use only; no confidential, personal, sensitive, regulated, child, employment, financial-account, health, or government-identifier data.
- **Technical boundary:** public HTTPS staging with same-origin assets. Protected previews, client VPNs, arbitrary scripts, and unsupported integrations are out of scope.
- **Case study:** separate written permission after the pilot. Participation does not imply permission to name the agency or its client.
- **Exit:** either side may end the pilot. Greenlit can pause high-impact capabilities for safety, capacity, or incident response.

## Founder price decision

Recommended for the first two design partners: a no-charge research pilot with no auto-renewal and no promise of future free service. This minimizes billing and refund complexity while the operating model is still being validated.

Before using that recommendation, the founder must choose and document one:

- No-charge 30-day research pilot.
- Fixed paid pilot with an exact amount, refund rule, tax treatment, invoice timing, and owner.
- A different written offer reviewed by counsel.

Do not use percentage-of-invoice pricing during the beta.

## Success decision after 30 days

Continue only if the pilot produces all of:

- at least one qualified milestone completes the proof flow;
- the client reviewer can make a decision without live assistance;
- the agency reports less approval-preparation or follow-up work;
- no severity-one or severity-two trust incident occurs;
- support effort and runner cost remain acceptable;
- the agency says it would repeat the workflow on an appropriate milestone.

## Not included

SOC 2, ISO, PCI, WCAG certification, legal e-signature status, payment collection, guaranteed invoice delivery or payment, custom integrations, protected preview bypasses, enterprise SSO, procurement commitments, SLAs, DPAs, BAAs, data residency promises, or 24/7 support.
