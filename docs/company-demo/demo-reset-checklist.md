# Demo reset checklist

Complete this 15 minutes before every external meeting.

## Product state

- Open `/workspace?demo=guided` in a fresh private window.
- Confirm the banner says synthetic/guided demo.
- Complete rc1, rc2, client review, approval, and sample receipt.
- Confirm both fixture images load and AC-04 shows the false-success frame.
- Clear the browser key `greenlit-demo-decision` by opening a fresh private window; do not inspect or clear unrelated browser storage.
- Keep `/receipt/demo` in a backup tab.

## Presentation

- Close personal tabs, messaging, password managers, terminals, dashboards, and notifications.
- Use a 1280×720 or larger display and verify 100% browser zoom.
- Keep `20-minute-runbook.md` on a second screen or printed.
- Open the approved screenshots from `apps/web/public/demo-evidence/`.
- Confirm camera, microphone, screen sharing, and power.

## Safety

- Confirm the demo uses no prospect SOW, credentials, access code, client name, or real email.
- Confirm the public support address and operator identity are correct.
- Confirm the private operator page loads with MFA.
- Confirm RUNS, REVIEWS, and INVOICES controls are available.
- If any production health check is red, use only the synthetic walkthrough.

## After the meeting

- Record fit, objections, promised follow-up, and owner in the approved CRM or internal system.
- Send the personalized follow-up within one business day.
- Do not create an invite until the offer, capacity, and data/technical fit are confirmed.
- Do not retain notes containing client SOW text, credentials, access codes, or regulated data.
