The problem

Agency teams prepare proof in one place, chase approval in another, and hand billing a decision with little connected context. Genuine fixes can blur into new scope.

The workflow

  1. Import the relevant SOW section.
  2. Confirm measurable criteria tied to exact source language.
  3. Verify supported promises against an authorized public staging build.
  4. Give one named reviewer the promise, result, evidence, and deadline.
  5. Retain the decision and hand the milestone into billing.
Why it is different

Greenlit keeps scope, browser observation, client decision, and the billing handoff connected. It does not treat a polished success message as proof when the underlying request failed.

Good first milestone

  • Recurring approval bottleneck
  • One named client reviewer
  • Public HTTPS staging with same-origin assets
  • Synthetic or authorized non-confidential content

Current boundaries

  • No regulated or confidential data
  • No protected-preview or VPN bypass
  • No signature, compliance, or payment guarantee
