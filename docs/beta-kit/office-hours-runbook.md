# Beta office-hours runbook

An operator must choose and attend the session. Do not publish a schedule until support coverage is confirmed.

## Recommended format

- Cadence: once per week during the first beta cohort.
- Length: 30 minutes.
- Audience: invited agency owners, project managers, designers, developers, and QA.
- Location: a business video-meeting account controlled by the operator.
- Recording: off by default. Obtain explicit consent before any recording.

## Agenda

1. Five minutes: material product or limitation changes.
2. Ten minutes: one synthetic workflow demonstration.
3. Ten minutes: agency questions and blockers.
4. Five minutes: next actions and where to report private issues.

## Opening statement

“This session is for product guidance and beta feedback. Do not share client SOW text, credentials, private review links, access codes, or regulated data on screen or in chat. We will move account-specific, privacy, security, or invoice issues to the monitored support channel.”

## Operator checklist

- [ ] Production health is ready before the session.
- [ ] Synthetic demo is available.
- [ ] Current known limitations are listed.
- [ ] Support channel is monitored.
- [ ] No customer workspace will be shown.
- [ ] Questions are recorded as product themes, not copied client data.
- [ ] Every action has an owner and date.

## After the session

- Publish a short summary with no participant or client information.
- Enter product issues through the normal triage path.
- Update FAQ or troubleshooting when a question repeats.
- Follow up privately on account-specific concerns.
