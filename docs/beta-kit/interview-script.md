# Greenlit beta interview script

Target length: 25 minutes

Do not defend the product or teach during the first part of the interview. Ask for specific recent behavior, not general opinions.

## Opening

“Thanks for testing Greenlit. I want to understand exactly what happened, including where the product failed or did not fit. I will take notes, but I will not include client SOW text or sensitive project information. Nothing from this conversation will be published without separate permission.”

## Context, 4 minutes

1. What type of agency work do you deliver?
2. What milestone did you try in Greenlit?
3. Who normally decides that this kind of milestone is complete?
4. Before Greenlit, how would you have requested and recorded the approval?

## Baseline, 4 minutes

1. How many review rounds does this kind of milestone usually take?
2. Roughly how much team time goes into preparing proof and chasing the decision?
3. How many days normally pass between finishing the work and receiving approval?
4. How many days pass between finishing work and sending the invoice?
5. Where do disagreements about fixes versus new scope usually occur?

## Product walkthrough, 8 minutes

1. Tell me what you expected when you first opened the workspace.
2. Which step required the most thought or outside help?
3. How did you decide whether each acceptance criterion was correct?
4. What did the verification result help you see, if anything?
5. Was any result confusing or untrustworthy? Why?
6. How did the client respond to the review?
7. Where did the workflow add work rather than remove it?
8. At what point, if any, did you consider abandoning the process?

## Value and fit, 5 minutes

1. What changed compared with your normal process?
2. Did Greenlit affect review rounds, decision time, invoice timing, or confidence?
3. Which part would you miss if access ended tomorrow?
4. Which existing tool should Greenlit connect to first?
5. What would have to be true for you to use this on the next suitable milestone?

## Close, 4 minutes

1. What is the most important problem Greenlit should fix before inviting more agencies?
2. Who else on your team should we observe using it?
3. May we contact you about another test?
4. Separately, would you consider approving an anonymous quote, named quote, logo, metric, or case study after reviewing the exact draft?
