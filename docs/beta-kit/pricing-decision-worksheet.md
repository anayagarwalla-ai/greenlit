# Greenlit pricing decision worksheet

Do not set public pricing from competitor pages alone. Use beta behavior, willingness-to-pay conversations, service cost, and the unit agencies naturally manage.

## Working recommendation for founder approval

Use this as the internal starting point for the first two design partners, not as published pricing:

- Free or paid: no-charge evaluation
- Cohort size: 2 agencies
- Duration: 30 days from kickoff
- Included agency members: 2
- Included client reviewers: 1 named reviewer per active milestone
- Included active milestones: 1 per agency
- Included retained runs: 8 per agency
- Included evidence retention: 30 days after the evaluation ends
- Stripe invoicing included: test mode only; do not collect payment
- Support level: scheduled office hours plus best-effort email support
- Renewal: no automatic renewal
- Expansion: a new written agreement after the evaluation

Founder and counsel must approve the commercial language, tax treatment, refund position, and any paid conversion before this recommendation is shown to a prospect.

## Interview questions

1. What does the approval problem cost today?
2. Which budget would pay for Greenlit?
3. Which usage limit would feel natural?
4. Which limit would discourage using Greenlit on the right projects?
5. What would make the product too risky to buy?
6. At what monthly price would it be an easy yes, require approval, or be rejected?
